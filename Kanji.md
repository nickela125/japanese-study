# Kanji

## Readings
- On'yomi 音読み: Readings derived from the Chinese pronunciations.
- Kun'yomi 訓読み: The original, indigenous Japanese readings.

### Which to use?
General Rules:

- On'yomi
    - Two-kanji (or more) compound (jukugo 熟語じゅくご)
    - Less common single kanji words, incluing numbers
- Kun'yomi
    - Single kanji words (most beginner nouns)
    - Kanji + hiragana (kana suffixes = okurigana 送り仮名おくがな) - mostly adjectives and verbs
    - Some kanji compounds words (esp nature or cardinal directions)
