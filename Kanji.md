# 🈶 Kanji

## 📖 Readings
- [Tofugu: On'yomi vs Kun'yomi](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese/onyomi-kunyomi/) - this article is worth reading to understand the different Kanji readings

- **On'yomi 音読み**: Readings derived from Chinese
- **Kun'yomi 訓読み:** The original, indigenous Japanese readings

### ❓ Which to use? ❓

## 🇨🇳 On'yomi (音読み) 🇨🇳
Used For:

- 📚 Compound Kanji words (Jukugo)
- 🔢 Numbers
- 🧠 More abstract or formal words

**Examples:**

| Word     | Reading      | Meaning        | Notes                            |
|----------|--------------|----------------|----------------------------------|
| 学校     | がっこう      | School          | Compound (学 + 校)               |
| 電話     | でんわ        | Telephone       | Compound (電 + 話)               |
| 三       | さん          | Three           | Number, uses On'yomi             |
| 研究     | けんきゅう    | Research        | Abstract concept                 |

> 🧠 Tip: On'yomi is often used when **kanji are stacked together with no hiragana** between.


## 🇯🇵 Kun'yomi (訓読み) 🇯🇵
Used for:
- 🧒 Single-kanji (beginner) words
- 📝 Verbs/adjectives with hiragana endings (okurigana = kana suffix)
- 🌿 Some compound words (esp nature or cardinal directions)

**Examples:**

| Word     | Reading      | Meaning        | Notes                               |
|----------|--------------|----------------|-------------------------------------|
| 山       | やま          | Mountain        | Simple word, no compound            |
| 見る     | みる          | To see          | Verb with okurigana (る)              |
| 大きい   | おおきい      | Big             | い-adjective, okurigana (きい)        |
| 東口     | ひがしぐち    | East exit       | Mix of Kun'yomi (東) + On'yomi (口) |

> 🧠 Tip: Kun'yomi is often used when **the kanji has hiragana attached** or appears on its own.

## Other Readings

### 🔁 Rendaku 🔁
[Tofugu: Rendaku](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese/rendaku/)

- Happens when 2+ words combined to a new one, and the initial consonant of the second word becomes "voiced" (it gets the little dakuten or handakuten mark) 

e.g. 人々 is ひとびと and not ひとひと

# 🧠 Vocab tips
Random things from Wani Kani

- 〜さ is exquivalent to -ness (e.g. 大きさ = bigness= size)
- ~した is past tense of する, so words ending with this are like "did ..." (e.g. 大した = did big = great)
- In a 2 kanji word, the 1st kanji sometimes has a possessive relationship with the 2nd (e.g. 王子 = king's child = prince)
