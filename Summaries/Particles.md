## 📝 Notes

### は
- The "topic particle" 
- Marks the topic in the sentence
- Emphasises what comes _after_ it
    - e.g. It's a big dog = これ は おおきい いぬ です
        - Emphasis is on "big dog", but let's us know we are talking about "this"

### が
- The "Subject Particle"
- Emphasises what comes _before_ it
    - e.g. _This_ is a big dog = これ が おおきい いぬ です
- Use with question words instead of は
    - e.g. だれ が いきます か？ = _Who_ will go? (v.s. as for who, _will_ go? Doesn't make sense)
