## 🧩 Grammar Notes

### 3️⃣ Particles
Nouns are generally followed by particles - indicate their relationships to the verbs.

#### ✨ を ✨
- Direct object - directly involved in / affected by the event

**Examples**
- コーヒー を <ruby>飲<rp>(</rp><rt>の</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>みます  

    _I drink coffee._
