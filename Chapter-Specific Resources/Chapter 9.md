### 6️⃣ Already...; Not ... Yet: もう〜ました and まだ〜ていません
 もう〜ました and まだ〜ていません (have already... and have not... yet)

#### ❓ Question ❓

>もう　(object)　（ました-verb) か。

= have you already (verbed) (object)?

#### ✅ Answer: Yes ✅

>はい、(object)　（ました-verb)。

= Yes, I have already (verbed) (object).

#### ❌ Answer: No ❌

>いいえ、まだ　(object)　(て-form verb) いません。

= No, I have not (verbed) (object) yet


#### ✨ Notes on もう (already) ✨
- Past event that still has an effect at present
- Use aff. past tense verb (〜ました) - same as events that happened in the past
- Applies to other frequency adverbs too
- Can put time reference (e.g. きょう before or after もう)

**Examples**
- きのうしゅくだいを<u>しました</u>。  

    _I did the homework yesterday (regular past tense)._

- もうしゅくだいをし<u>ました</u>。

    _I have already done the homework._

#### 🐢 Notes on まだ (not yet) 🐢
- How things stand now
- Use neg. present ている - c.f. 〜ませんでした (only used for a finished time period)

**Examples**
- きのうしゅくだいをし<u>ませんでした</u>。 

    _I did not do the homework yesterday (regular negative past)._

- まだしゅくだいをし<u>ていません</u>。

    _I have not done the homework yet._
