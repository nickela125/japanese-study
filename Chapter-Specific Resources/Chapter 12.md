## 🧩 Grammar Notes

### 2️⃣ 〜すきる(too much)

>verb stem / adj stem + すきる

= ... too much

>verb / adj short neg ~~い~~ + さすきる
>adj

= not ... enough (or maybe not ... too much)

#### ✨ Notes ✨

- Verb stem = Verb ~~ます~~
- Adjective stem 
    - い-adj = adj-~~い ~~
    - な-adj = adj-~~な~~
- すきる  conjugates like a る-verb
- Used when something is beyond normal or proper
    - Not for compliments (e.g. "too kind" should use とても / すごく)
- Can also use the noun すぎ
    - e.g. たべすぎです = eating too much

#### 📝 Examples 📝
- は<ruby>早<rp>(</rp><rt>はや</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>く<ruby>起<rp>(</rp><rt>お</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>きすぎました。
    - I got up too early.

- たべなさぎる
    - I didn't eat enough
- たかくなさすぎる
    - It is not expensive enough (or it is not too expensive)
- しずかじゃなさすぎる
    - It's not quiet enough

### 3️⃣ 〜ほうがいいです (had better)

> verb (short, past / た) + ほうがいいです

= You had better... / You really should...

> verb (short, pres, neg / ない) + ほうがいいです

=  You had better not... / You really shouldn't...

#### ✨ Notes ✨

- When someone really should (or shouldn't) do something
- Lit: "it would be better if you..."
- Often followed with よ - "new information"
- Can use ほうがいい**んです**(よ) to add more emphasis

#### 📝 Examples 📝
- もっと<ruby>野<rp>(</rp><rt>や</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby><ruby>菜<rp>(</rp><rt>さい</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>を<ruby>食<rp>(</rp><rt>た</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>べたほうがいいですよ。
    - You'd better eat more vegetables.
- も<ruby>授<rp>(</rp><rt>じゅ</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby><ruby>業<rp>(</rp><rt>ぎょう</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>を<ruby>休<rp>(</rp><rt>やす</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>まないほうがいいですよ。
    - It is better not to skip classes.
- <ruby>食<rp>(</rp><rt>た</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>べぎないほうがいいですよ。
    - You'd better not eat too much.

### 4️⃣ 〜ので

>(reason ので) (situation)。

> short-form + ので、______

= (situation), _because_ (reason).

#### ✨ Notes ✨
- Short-form includes な for な-adj and noun (no だ for noun)
- ので is for reasons that are "valid, evident and acceptable" to the listener (e.g. it is raining, or it is espensive (and your audience agrees))
- Can use long form to be really polite (usually short form)

#### 📝 Examples 📝
- あした<ruby>試<rp>(</rp><rt>し</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby><ruby>験<rp>(</rp><rt>けん</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>がある ので、<ruby>今<rp>(</rp><rt>こん</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby><ruby>晩<rp>(</rp><rt>ばん</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby><ruby>勉<rp>(</rp><rt>べん</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby><ruby>強<rp>(</rp><rt>きょう</rt><rp>)</rp><ruby>します。
    - I will study this evening, (because) we will have an exam tomorrow.
