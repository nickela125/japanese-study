## Grammar Notes

### 6. Verb のがすきです / じょうずです (like doing / good at [verb])
```
                                         | すき　　　|         likes doing...
person は activity (short form verb) のが | きらい　　|  です　　　doesn't like doing...
                                         | じょうず　|         is good at...
                                         | へた　　　|         is poor at doing...
```

- Short form verb + の = noun describing an action
- Use to describe verb you like / dislike / are good at / are poor at

_Examples_
- (わたしは) にほんごをべんきょうするのがすきです。
    - _I like studying the Japanese language._
- (わたしは) へやをそうじするのがきらいです。
    - _I don't like cleaning my room._
- ロバートさんはりょうりをつくるのがじょうずです。
    - _Robert is good at cooking meals._
- たけしさんはえいごをはなすのがへたです。
    - _Takeshi is not a good speaker of English._
